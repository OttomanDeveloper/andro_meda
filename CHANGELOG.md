## [1.0.0] - 2026-05-30

### Added

- Nine custom-painted eras spanning the Big Bang to the future, plus a developer
  portfolio reveal.
- Scroll-driven timeline with a tap-to-jump progress bar.
- Interactive moments: drag to spin galaxies, tap to ignite stars, and tap or
  hover to reveal the dinosaur titans.
- Responsive mobile, tablet, and desktop layouts with touch-first interactions.
- Visibility-gated per-frame animation via `EraScope` for performance.
- Open-source project files: README, CONTRIBUTING, license (CC BY 4.0),
  issue/PR templates, and a CI workflow.
